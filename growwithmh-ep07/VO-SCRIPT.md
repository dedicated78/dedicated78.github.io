- **`vo-sequenced.wav`:** the voiceover in film order, 48 kHz.

  - A bright major bed that gains energy as the name grows.

  - A quiet return under the explanation, and a brighter bed for the punchline.

  - idea chime

  - crown sparkle and a rising tension tone

  - a ribbon whoosh and the assistant's rise
  - result-card flips, the wrap whoosh and the caution notification
  - lesson whooshes, field ticks and check clicks
  - website card flips, typing and the final full-stop tick
  - statement hit and sonic logo
- **`master.wav`:** the final mix. Music is −10 dB and sidechain-ducked under the VO, SFX are −1 dB, loudnorm targets −14 LUFS, and a limiter follows.
