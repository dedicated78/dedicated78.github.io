## Character animation notes
- **Rig:** the Owner and the Maps Assistant are the approved rigs, unchanged in design. Four small rig additions were made, all in the existing style and drawn in head or hand space so they track every head tilt and arm pose:
  - **Tears:** pooled tears on the lower lids, a wet streak, and drops sliding down each cheek.
  - **Sad brows:** an inner-brow lift.
  - **Bouquet in the fist:** built into the hand like the Ep02 phone, counter-rotated to stay upright, with the fist drawn over the stems.
  - **Hip hinge:** for the bow. The legs stay planted and vertical while the upper body leans.
- **Bouquet hand-off:** the resting bouquet is placed from the hand's computed position at the release frame, so it does not jump, float or slide. The plinth height is derived from that same point, so the stems touch the plinth top.
- **Kneeling:** none. The brief allowed simplifying if kneeling would look unnatural, so it is a slow bow and reach.

## Fact-check
- **How Google determines local ranking** (support.google.com/business/answer/7091), read 2026-11-15:
  - "Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence."
  - Prominence "is also based on info like how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have. More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking."
  - Distance is "how far each business is from the customer who's searching."
  - Relevance is "how well a Business Profile matches what someone is searching for."
  - **What the episode avoids saying:** that reviews don't matter, that any factor guarantees a ranking, or that the factors are equally weighted.
- **Businesses in ChatGPT:** see Limitations.
  - **First-hand read:** not possible. openai.com refused automated access from this environment (a Cloudflare challenge).
  - **Corroboration:** the claims are backed by a web search that surfaced OpenAI's announcement page title and description ("We're introducing Businesses in ChatGPT in beta"; Business Hub to claim a business), plus independent coverage dated 10–11 November 2026. That coverage includes technology.org (2026/11/11), Quartz and fonearena.
    - It reports: claiming is open to eligible businesses; verified businesses can update key details; Business Agents are a separate feature, limited at launch; and no visibility guarantee.
  - **On screen:** the text follows the brief's wording and safeguards, and does not mention Business Agents.

## Limitations
1. **OpenAI facts were not read first-hand.**
   - openai.com blocks automated fetches from this environment with a Cloudflare challenge. I added openai.com to the allowlist; the site still refused.
   - A browser-based workaround was blocked by the session's safety classifier, so I did not pursue it.
   - The news text is corroborated by a web search: OpenAI's own page snippet, plus coverage dated 10–11 Nov 2026. Please open https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/ once and confirm the date, "beta", and "claim → verify → update".
2. **The ChatGPT logo cameo is a trademark use.**
   - It is the supplied logo, unaltered, with stick limbs added, tagged UNOFFICIAL CAMEO, and the news card adds no endorsement.
   - OpenAI's brand guidelines restrict modifying or animating its marks. Adding limbs may fall under that even as parody.
   - Your call before publishing. A plain logo card without limbs is a 10-minute swap.
3. **One voice for three roles.** "Jack John" plays the owner, the assistant and the narrator; they are distinct by pitch.
4. **No kneel.** The flowers are laid with a hip bow and reach instead of a kneel, as the brief allowed.
5. **Sound is synthesized in-project.** The sobbing is limited to two soft sniffs so it doesn't fight the dialogue. The violin is a synthesized bowed-string voice, not a recorded violin.
